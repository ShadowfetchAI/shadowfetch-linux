# Shadowfetch App Shelf Curation Brief

## Purpose

The App Shelf supports the Linux-first Shadowfetch identity. It proves shipping ability without competing with Shadowfetch Linux as the flagship product.

## Framing copy

The App Shelf is Shadowfetch’s field kit: small tools for daily jobs, built with the same bias as the workstation — practical, private, and fast.

## Categories

- Documents
- Privacy
- Travel
- Recipes
- Caregiving
- Calculators
- Utilities
- Everyday tools

## Featured-pick criteria

Select 12 apps that satisfy most of these:

- The app name is instantly understandable.
- The icon is clean at small sizes.
- The App Store link works.
- The privacy-policy link works.
- The app solves a real daily job in one sentence.
- Screenshots do not look stale or broken.
- The app strengthens the “we ship focused tools” proof point.

## Featured-pick template

```md
### App name
- Category:
- One-line value:
- App Store URL:
- Privacy URL:
- Screenshot status:
- Keep / fix / remove from featured shelf:
```

## Do not do

- Do not call the Shelf the main product.
- Do not inflate download numbers.
- Do not imply every app is strategically equal.
- Do not bury Linux CTAs under app promotion.
